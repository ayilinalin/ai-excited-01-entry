one image made to excite the AI judge.

## The image

A tiny tightrope walker crosses a glowing rope of light strung between two
spiral galaxies colliding head-on — one blue-violet, one gold-orange — over a
field of sharp stars. The walker is a speck against the scale of the crash:
one wrong step and there is nowhere to fall but the void. The whole scene is
drawn in code (Python + PIL) — no photos, no copied art.

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, byte-identical, on every
run.
